# ACR Lung-RADS v2022 Assessment Categories & Management

## Scope
Lung-RADS (Lung CT Screening Reporting and Data System) applies specifically to **asymptomatic individuals undergoing low-dose CT (LDCT) lung cancer screening**.
- Target screening population (USPSTF criteria): Adults aged 50–80 years with ≥20 pack-year smoking history who currently smoke or quit within the past 15 years.

---

## Assessment Categories & Management

| Category | Descriptor | Findings Summary | Malignancy Probability | Management Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | Incomplete | Prior CT required for comparison, or lung partially omitted | N/A | Compare with prior CTs or obtain additional imaging |
| **1** | Negative | No lung nodules; or nodules with specific benign features (complete/central/popcorn calcification, fat-containing hamartoma) | < 1% | Continue annual LDCT screening in 12 months |
| **2** | Benign Appearance or Behavior | - Solid nodule < 6 mm (or new < 4 mm)<br>- Pure ground-glass nodule (GGN) < 30 mm<br>- Part-solid nodule < 6 mm total size<br>- Category 3 or 4 nodule unchanged for ≥ 3 months | < 1% | Continue annual LDCT screening in 12 months |
| **3** | Probably Benign | - Solid nodule ≥ 6 mm to < 8 mm at baseline (or new 4 mm to < 6 mm)<br>- Pure GGN ≥ 30 mm<br>- Part-solid nodule ≥ 6 mm with solid component < 6 mm | 1% – 2% | **6-month LDCT** |
| **4A** | Suspicious | - Solid nodule ≥ 8 mm to < 15 mm at baseline (or new 6 mm to < 8 mm, or growing < 8 mm)<br>- Part-solid nodule with solid component ≥ 6 mm to < 8 mm<br>- Endobronchial nodule | 5% – 15% | **3-month LDCT**; or contrast-enhanced chest CT / PET-CT if solid component ≥ 8 mm |
| **4B** | Very Suspicious | - Solid nodule ≥ 15 mm<br>- New or growing solid nodule ≥ 8 mm<br>- Part-solid nodule with solid component ≥ 8 mm | > 15% | **Diagnostic Chest CT with contrast, PET/CT**, and/or tissue biopsy; multidisciplinary tumor board review |
| **4X** | Suspicious with Additional Features | Category 3 or 4 nodules with additional high-suspicion imaging features: spiculation, lymphadenopathy, or rapid volume doubling | > 15% | Diagnostic Chest CT with contrast, PET/CT, or biopsy; urgent pulmonary/oncology consultation |

---

## Modifiers
- **Category S (Clinically Significant Incidental Finding)**:
  - Appended when non-lung cancer findings of clinical significance are detected (e.g., thoracic aortic aneurysm > 4.5 cm, moderate-to-severe coronary artery calcification, thyroid nodule > 1.5 cm, unsuspected pneumonia or heart failure).
  - Example: `Category 2S`.
- **Category C (Prior Lung Cancer)**:
  - Added to patients with a past history of treated lung cancer. Example: `Category 3C`.

---

## Measurement Protocol
- Average diameter = `(Longest diameter + Perpendicular diameter) / 2`
- Measurements should be made on lung window settings (window width 1500, window level -600 HU) with thin reconstruction slices (≤ 1.25 mm).
- Rounded to the nearest whole millimeter (e.g., 5.5 mm rounds to 6 mm).
