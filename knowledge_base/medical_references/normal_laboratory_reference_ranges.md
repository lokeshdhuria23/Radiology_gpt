# Normal Laboratory Reference Ranges & Critical Value Alert Thresholds

## 1. Complete Blood Count (CBC) with Differential

| Test Parameter | Conventional Reference Range | SI Units Reference Range | Critical Low Alert | Critical High Alert | Clinical Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **White Blood Cells (WBC)** | 4.5 – 11.0 × 10³/µL | 4.5 – 11.0 × 10⁹/L | < 2.0 × 10³/µL | > 30.0 × 10³/µL | Leukopenia (neutropenia risk) vs. leukemoid reaction / leukemia |
| **Hemoglobin (Male)** | 13.8 – 17.2 g/dL | 138 – 172 g/L | < 7.0 g/dL | > 20.0 g/dL | Transfusion threshold typically < 7.0 g/dL (< 8.0 with CAD) |
| **Hemoglobin (Female)** | 12.1 – 15.1 g/dL | 121 – 151 g/L | < 7.0 g/dL | > 20.0 g/dL | Anemia evaluation: microcytic (iron/thalassemia), macrocytic (B12/folate) |
| **Hematocrit (Male)** | 40.7 – 50.3 % | 0.40 – 0.50 | < 21.0 % | > 60.0 % | Polycythemia vera vs. dehydration vs. severe blood loss |
| **Hematocrit (Female)** | 36.1 – 44.3 % | 0.36 – 0.44 | < 21.0 % | > 60.0 % | Hemoconcentration in burn / shock |
| **Platelets** | 150 – 450 × 10³/µL | 150 – 450 × 10⁹/L | < 50 × 10³/µL | > 1,000 × 10³/µL | Spontaneous bleeding risk < 10-20k; thrombocytosis risk in malignancy |
| **Absolute Neutrophil (ANC)**| 1.8 – 7.0 × 10³/µL | 1.8 – 7.0 × 10⁹/L | < 0.5 × 10³/µL | — | Severe neutropenia (< 500/µL) = neutropenic fever emergency |

## 2. Basic & Comprehensive Metabolic Panel (BMP / CMP)

| Analyte | Standard Range | Critical Low | Critical High | High-Value Clinical Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sodium (Na+)** | 135 – 145 mEq/L | < 120 mEq/L | > 160 mEq/L | Severe hyponatremia: Risk of cerebral edema / seizures; correct slowly (max 8 mEq/L/24h to avoid ODS) |
| **Potassium (K+)** | 3.5 – 5.0 mEq/L | < 2.8 mEq/L | > 6.0 mEq/L | **Cardiac Arrythmia Alert**: High risk of VFib / arrest; obtain emergent 12-lead ECG; IV calcium gluconate if peaked T-waves |
| **Chloride (Cl-)** | 96 – 106 mEq/L | < 80 mEq/L | > 115 mEq/L | Metabolic alkalosis vs. hyperchloremic non-gap acidosis |
| **Bicarbonate (CO2)** | 22 – 29 mEq/L | < 10 mEq/L | > 40 mEq/L | Low: Metabolic acidosis (calculate Anion Gap = Na - [Cl + HCO3], normal 8-12) |
| **Blood Urea Nitrogen (BUN)**| 7 – 20 mg/dL | — | > 100 mg/dL | Elevated BUN/Cr ratio > 20:1 suggests prerenal azotemia or GI bleed |
| **Creatinine (Male)** | 0.7 – 1.3 mg/dL | — | > 4.0 mg/dL | Sharp rise indicates AKI; baseline calculation needed for eGFR |
| **Creatinine (Female)** | 0.6 – 1.1 mg/dL | — | > 4.0 mg/dL | Female reference lower due to baseline muscle mass differences |
| **eGFR (CKD-EPI)** | > 90 mL/min/1.73m²| < 30 mL/min | — | **Radiology Hold**: eGFR < 30 triggers iodinated contrast review & metformin hold |
| **Glucose (Fasting)** | 70 – 99 mg/dL | < 50 mg/dL | > 400 mg/dL | Hypoglycemia emergency: IV D50W / Glucagon. High: DKA / HHS workup |
| **Calcium (Total)** | 8.5 – 10.5 mg/dL | < 6.5 mg/dL | > 13.0 mg/dL | Correct for albumin: `Corrected Ca = Total Ca + 0.8 * (4.0 - Albumin)` |
| **Total Bilirubin** | 0.2 – 1.2 mg/dL | — | > 15.0 mg/dL | Jaundice visible when bilirubin > 2.5–3.0 mg/dL |
| **Aspartate Aminotransferase (AST)**| 10 – 40 U/L| — | > 1,000 U/L | Marked elevation (>1000): Ischemic hepatitis, acetaminophen toxicity, acute viral |
| **Alanine Aminotransferase (ALT)**| 7 – 56 U/L | — | > 1,000 U/L | More specific for hepatic parenchymal injury than AST |
| **Alkaline Phosphatase (ALP)**| 44 – 147 U/L | — | > 500 U/L | Biliary obstruction vs. high bone turnover (Paget's, bone metastasis) |

## 3. Cardiac Biomarkers & Coagulation Profile

| Test | Normal Range | Abnormal / Critical Threshold | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **High-Sensitivity Troponin I (hs-cTnI)** | < 14 ng/L (Female), < 26 ng/L (Male) | > 50 ng/L or >20% delta at 1-2 hours | Myocardial injury / Acute Coronary Syndrome |
| **B-Type Natriuretic Peptide (BNP)** | < 100 pg/mL | > 400 pg/mL | High probability of acute congestive heart failure |
| **NT-proBNP** | < 300 pg/mL | > 900 pg/mL (age 50-75) | Rule-in threshold for decompensated heart failure |
| **Prothrombin Time (PT)** | 11.0 – 13.5 seconds | > 20.0 seconds | Extrinsic pathway; prolonged in liver disease, vitamin K deficiency |
| **International Normalized Ratio (INR)** | 0.8 – 1.1 (Non-anticoagulated) | > 4.5 (High bleed risk); > 5.0 (Critical) | Therapeutic target for DVT/PE/AFib is 2.0 – 3.0 (Mechanical valve 2.5 – 3.5) |
| **Activated Partial Thromboplastin (aPTT)**| 25 – 35 seconds | > 100 seconds | Intrinsic pathway; therapeutic unfractionated heparin monitoring (1.5-2.5x control) |
| **D-Dimer** | < 500 ng/mL FEU (or age × 10 if >50yo)| > 500 ng/mL FEU | High negative predictive value for ruling out DVT/PE when pre-test score is low |
| **Lactate** | 0.5 – 2.0 mmol/L | > 4.0 mmol/L | Tissue hypoperfusion; hallmark of severe sepsis, septic shock, or mesenteric ischemia |
