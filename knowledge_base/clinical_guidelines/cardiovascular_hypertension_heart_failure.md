# ACC/AHA Clinical Guidelines: Hypertension and Heart Failure

## Part I: Hypertension Guidelines (ACC/AHA 2017/2023)

### Blood Pressure Classification (Adults)
| Category | Systolic (mmHg) | & / or | Diastolic (mmHg) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Normal** | < 120 | and | < 80 |
| **Elevated** | 120–129 | and | < 80 |
| **Stage 1 Hypertension** | 130–139 | or | 80–89 |
| **Stage 2 Hypertension** | ≥ 140 | or | ≥ 90 |
| **Hypertensive Crisis** | > 180 | and/or | > 120 |

### Treatment Thresholds & Targets
- **Target BP**: < 130/80 mmHg for all adult patients with confirmed hypertension, including those with chronic kidney disease (CKD), diabetes mellitus, and established coronary disease.
- **Stage 1**:
  - 10-year ASCVD risk < 10%: Nonpharmacologic therapy (DASH diet, sodium reduction <1500 mg/d, exercise, weight reduction); recheck in 3–6 months.
  - 10-year ASCVD risk ≥ 10% or comorbid CKD/Diabetes: Initiate 1 first-line antihypertensive medication.
- **Stage 2**:
  - Average BP > 20/10 mmHg above goal: Prompt initiation of **2 first-line agents of different classes** (e.g., ACEi/ARB + DHP-CCB).

### First-Line Antihypertensive Drug Classes
1. **Thiazide / Thiazide-like Diuretics**: Chlorthalidone (12.5–25 mg daily preferred over Hydrochlorothiazide).
2. **ACE Inhibitors (ACEi)**: Lisinopril, Enalapril, Ramipril. (Contraindicated in pregnancy and bilateral renal artery stenosis).
3. **Angiotensin Receptor Blockers (ARBs)**: Losartan, Valsartan, Candesartan. (Do NOT combine ACEi + ARB due to severe hyperkalemia and renal failure risk).
4. **Dihydropyridine Calcium Channel Blockers (CCBs)**: Amlodipine, Nifedipine ER. (Watch for peripheral edema).

## Part II: Heart Failure with Reduced Ejection Fraction (HFrEF, LVEF ≤ 40%)

### Guideline-Directed Medical Therapy (GDMT - The "Four Pillars")
Every HFrEF patient should be titrated onto all four foundational classes unless contraindicated:

1. **ARNI (Angiotensin Receptor-Neprilysin Inhibitor) / ACEi / ARB**:
   - **Sacubitril/Valsartan**: Preferred over ACEi/ARB to reduce cardiovascular death and hospitalizations.
   - *Mandatory Washout*: 36-hour washout required when switching from ACE inhibitor to ARNI to prevent life-threatening angioedema.
2. **Beta-Blockers (Evidence-based only)**:
   - Metoprolol Succinate ER, Carvedilol, or Bisoprolol.
   - *Rule*: Initiate only when patient is euvolemic (not in acute decompensation).
3. **Mineralocorticoid Receptor Antagonists (MRA)**:
   - Spironolactone (25 mg daily) or Eplerenone.
   - *Monitoring*: Check serum potassium and creatinine at 1, 4, and 12 weeks. Hold if K+ > 5.0 mEq/L or eGFR < 30 mL/min.
4. **SGLT2 Inhibitors**:
   - Dapagliflozin (10 mg daily) or Empagliflozin (10 mg daily).
   - Proven mortality benefit regardless of diabetes status.

### Acute Decompensated Heart Failure (ADHF)
- **Clinical Presentation**: Dyspnea on exertion, orthopnea, paroxysmal nocturnal dyspnea, elevated JVP, bilateral crackles, peripheral pitting edema, S3 gallop.
- **Diagnostic Markers**:
  - **BNP > 100 pg/mL** or **NT-proBNP > 300 pg/mL** (age-adjusted: >450 if <50yo, >900 if 50-75yo, >1800 if >75yo).
- **Chest Radiograph Findings**:
  - Cardiomegaly (cardiothoracic ratio > 0.50)
  - Cephalization of pulmonary vessels (pulmonary venous hypertension)
  - Kerley B lines (interlobular interstitial edema)
  - Bat-wing perihilar alveolar infiltrates
  - Bilateral pleural effusions (transudative, right typically > left).
- **Acute Management**:
  - Intravenous loop diuretics (Furosemide IV dose equal to 2–2.5x oral outpatient daily dose).
  - Supplemental oxygen (target SpO2 ≥ 90%); non-invasive positive pressure ventilation (BiPAP/CPAP) for acute pulmonary edema.
