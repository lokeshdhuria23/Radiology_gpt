# American Diabetes Association (ADA) Standards of Care: Diabetes Management

## 1. Diagnostic Criteria for Diabetes Mellitus
| Test | Normal | Prediabetes | Diabetes Mellitus |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fasting Plasma Glucose (FPG)** | < 100 mg/dL (5.6 mmol/L) | 100–125 mg/dL | ≥ 126 mg/dL (7.0 mmol/L) |
| **2-hour Oral Glucose Tolerance (OGTT)** | < 140 mg/dL | 140–199 mg/dL | ≥ 200 mg/dL (11.1 mmol/L) |
| **Hemoglobin A1c (HbA1c)** | < 5.7% | 5.7% – 6.4% | ≥ 6.5% (48 mmol/mol) |
| **Random Plasma Glucose** | — | — | ≥ 200 mg/dL with classic hyperglycemic symptoms |

*Confirmation*: In the absence of unequivocal hyperglycemia with acute metabolic decompensation, diagnosis requires two abnormal test results from the same sample or two separate test samples.

---

## 2. Glycemic Goals
- **Adult Non-pregnant Patients**:
  - **HbA1c Target**: < 7.0% (53 mmol/mol) for most non-pregnant adults without significant hypoglycemia.
  - **Preprandial Capillary Glucose**: 80–130 mg/dL (4.4–7.2 mmol/L).
  - **Peak Postprandial Glucose**: < 180 mg/dL (10.0 mmol/L).
- **Less Stringent Goals (HbA1c < 8.0% or 8.5%)**:
  - History of severe hypoglycemia, limited life expectancy, advanced microvascular/macrovascular complications, extensive comorbid conditions, or frail elderly.

## 3. Pharmacotherapy for Type 2 Diabetes

### First-Line Agent: Metformin
- **Mechanism**: Decreases hepatic glucose production, decreases intestinal absorption, improves insulin sensitivity.
- **Starting Dose**: 500 mg once or twice daily with meals; titrate to maximum effective dose 2000 mg/day (1000 mg BID).
- **Renal Dosing & Safety Rules**:
  - Obtain baseline eGFR prior to initiation.
  - **eGFR 45–59 mL/min/1.73m²**: Can continue; monitor renal function every 3–6 months. Max dose 1000–1500 mg.
  - **eGFR 30–44 mL/min/1.73m²**: Do not initiate; if already taking, reduce dose by 50% (max 1000 mg/day).
  - **eGFR < 30 mL/min/1.73m²**: **CONTRAINDICATED** due to high risk of fatal Metformin-Associated Lactic Acidosis (MALA).
  - **Iodinated Radiologic Contrast Hold**:
    - Discontinue metformin at the time of or prior to iodinated contrast procedures in patients with eGFR < 60 mL/min, history of hepatic disease, alcoholism, or acute heart failure.
    - Re-evaluate eGFR 48 hours post-procedure; restart only if renal function has remained stable.

### Second-Line & Organ-Protective Agents
- **Patients with Atherosclerotic Cardiovascular Disease (ASCVD)**:
  - **GLP-1 Receptor Agonists (GLP-1 RA)** with proven CVD benefit: Dulaglutide, Semaglutide, Liraglutide.
- **Patients with Heart Failure (HFrEF/HFpEF) or Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD)**:
  - **SGLT2 Inhibitors**: Empagliflozin, Dapagliflozin, Canagliflozin.
  - Renal threshold: Can be initiated down to eGFR 20 mL/min for renal/cardiac protection.
- **Hypoglycemia Risk Classes**:
  - **Sulfonylureas (Glipizide, Glimepiride)**: Significant hypoglycemia risk; avoid Glyburide in renal impairment.
  - **Insulin**: Titrated basal insulin (Glargine, Degludec) when HbA1c remains >1.5-2.0% above target or in symptomatic catabolism (weight loss, polyuria).
