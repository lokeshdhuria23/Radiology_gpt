# KDIGO Clinical Guidelines: Chronic Kidney Disease & Acute Kidney Injury

## 1. CKD Classification & Staging (G & A Categories)

### GFR Categories (mL/min/1.73 m²)
| Stage | GFR Category | Description | Clinical Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G1** | ≥ 90 | Normal or high | Normal if no albuminuria/structural pathology |
| **G2** | 60–89 | Mildly decreased | Monitor annual eGFR; cardiovascular risk assessment |
| **G3a** | 45–59 | Mildly to moderately decreased | Adjust drug dosing; monitor every 6 months; monitor bone mineral markers |
| **G3b** | 30–44 | Moderately to severely decreased | Adjust nephrotoxic medications; monitor every 3-4 months |
| **G4** | 15–29 | Severely decreased | Nephrology referral; prepare for kidney replacement therapy (KRT) |
| **G5** | < 15 | Kidney failure | Dialysis or renal transplantation |

### Albuminuria Categories (UACR: Urine Albumin-to-Creatinine Ratio)
| Stage | AER (mg/24h) | UACR (mg/g) | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A1** | < 30 | < 30 | Normal to mildly increased |
| **A2** | 30–300 | 30–300 | Moderately increased (microalbuminuria) |
| **A3** | > 300 | > 300 | Severely increased (nephrotic range if >2200 mg/g) |

## 2. Acute Kidney Injury (KDIGO AKI Criteria)
Defined by any of the following:
- Increase in Serum Creatinine (SCr) by **≥ 0.3 mg/dL (≥ 26.5 µmol/L)** within 48 hours; OR
- Increase in SCr to **≥ 1.5 times baseline**, known or presumed to have occurred within the prior 7 days; OR
- Urine volume **< 0.5 mL/kg/h for 6 hours**.

### AKI Staging
| Stage | Serum Creatinine Threshold | Urine Output Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| **Stage 1** | 1.5–1.9x baseline OR ≥0.3 mg/dL increase | <0.5 mL/kg/h for 6–12 hours |
| **Stage 2** | 2.0–2.9x baseline | <0.5 mL/kg/h for ≥12 hours |
| **Stage 3** | 3.0x baseline OR SCr ≥4.0 mg/dL OR initiation of RRT | <0.3 mL/kg/h for ≥24 hours OR anuria for ≥12 hours |

## 3. Contrast-Associated Acute Kidney Injury (CA-AKI)
- **Risk Assessment**: Pre-existing CKD (baseline eGFR < 30 mL/min/1.73m² or unstable AKI), diabetes mellitus, hypovolemia, high-osmolar contrast media, and large contrast volume (>100 mL).
- **Prevention Strategy**:
  - **Volume Expansion**: Isotonic intravenous 0.9% normal saline (1 mL/kg/h for 3-4 hours pre- and 4-6 hours post-procedure).
  - **Lowest Diagnostic Volume**: Use low-osmolar or iso-osmolar nonionic contrast media.
  - **Avoid Repeat Contrast**: Delay second contrast study by minimum 48-72 hours when possible.
- **Nephrotoxic Drug Holds**:
  - Hold NSAIDs, aminoglycosides, and vancomycin if alternatives exist.
  - Temporarily hold ACE inhibitors/ARBs on the day of contrast in dehydrated or high-risk patients.
